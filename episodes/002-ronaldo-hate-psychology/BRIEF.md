# First release — Why Do Some Messi Fans Hate Ronaldo More Than They Love Messi?

Episode folder ID 002 is retained from the imported draft; release order is 1. The older 001 Messi-backlash project remains intact.

## Thesis

Creator's interpretation: Ronaldo's openly displayed ambition and status make him an unusually effective target for envy and rivalry. A quieter, commercially promoted Messi persona can be granted moral authority that should be questioned. Neither psychology nor marketing alone establishes match fixing.

## Audience and promise

Football fans who are tired of every technical debate becoming a judgment about someone's personality. Start with a recognizable hypothetical reaction, explain envy and identity, show a documented institution speaking in personality stereotypes, then follow the commercial incentives.
