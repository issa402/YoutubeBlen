# Recording the first release

## Files and timing

- `NARRATION.txt`: spoken text only; 1,416 whitespace-separated words. This is the recording copy.
- `NARRATION.md`: the same words, separated into nine readable chapters. Do not read the headings or introductory instruction.
- `MASTER_PRODUCTION_SCRIPT.md`: identical voiceover plus evidence and visual instructions.
- `OPENING_NARRATION.txt`: the first chapter only, exactly 80 words. Do not append it to the full narration; it is already included there.
- At 145 words per minute, the full read takes about 9 minutes 46 seconds before additional pauses. Aim for a finished read of 9–11 minutes. Measure the recording before locking edit points or public chapter timestamps.

## Delivery

Talk to one football fan across the table. You believe the argument. Do not perform a newsreader voice or shout through every line.

The opening is amused and incredulous. Let the question land. Start the status section with admiration for Ronaldo, then make the envy interpretation directly. The studies explain the argument; give them less theatrical emphasis. The Blatter section should sound frustrated by a concrete example. The business section is skeptical. Finish with conviction and a quieter final line.

Emphasize “his failure” and “your victory” in the opening. Later, emphasize “consistently” when discussing standards and “visible ambition” when explaining Ronaldo's appeal. Read “some fans” naturally once; do not turn every interpretation into a separate disclaimer.

The source-backed limits are part of the intended argument. Keep the baseball-study context, Miami's regular-season sporting case, and Ronaldo's sponsorship statement. They make the examples precise. Do not improvise a corruption allegation into a factual statement during the read.

Pause briefly between paragraphs and leave about one second after the final line. Record a second take of the opening and ending; use the more natural take. Use the familiar pronunciation of Messi and Ronaldo; pronounce schadenfreude approximately “SHAH-den-froy-duh.” Read MLS as individual letters. “2025” can be spoken as “twenty twenty-five.”

## Capture and handoff

Record a clean mono WAV at 48 kHz if your recorder supports it. Keep the microphone position steady and turn off loud room noise. Leave headroom so emphatic words do not clip. Record a few seconds of room tone separately.

Keep the original recording untouched. Create a separate edit for mistakes and long gaps. Save generated voice tracks and personal recordings in the ignored `.studio/` output area, and share only through the agreed transfer method. An offline synthetic voice is useful for testing timing; it does not establish the creator's final voice or approval.

After the read, set chapter starts from the actual audio and update the publishing pack. Match the first three animation cues to the opening waveform. Listen through the assembled video for missing words, abrupt cuts and whether the last line has room to finish.
