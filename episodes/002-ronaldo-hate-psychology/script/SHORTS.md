## Short 1: The rival became the main character

Imagine Messi has just done something brilliant. You go online to enjoy it. Five minutes later you're watching Ronaldo miss chances. How did that become the celebration? My take is that some fans get so invested in winning the argument that they forget to enjoy the player. A rival's failure starts feeling more useful than their favorite's success. You don't have to like Ronaldo. But if hating him is the best part of supporting Messi, Ronaldo has become the main character.

## Short 2: Judge the goal

Ronaldo is tall, glamorous, openly ambitious, and willing to tell you he thinks he's the best. I think that package gets under some people's skin. For one viewer it's inspiring. For another it's arrogance. But here's the test: does your football argument still work if you remove his face and his celebration? If you think he's overrated, make that case. Don't tell me his haircut invalidates a goal.

## Short 3: Promotion deserves questions

When Messi arrived in Miami, MLS and Apple announced special coverage around his arrival and enhanced production for his matches. That's documented commercial promotion. My point is that you should be able to admire the player and still question the machinery around him. Calling every skeptic jealous doesn't answer those questions. But promotion also doesn't prove a fixed match. Ask the specific question. Show the specific evidence.
