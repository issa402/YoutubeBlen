## Comparison limits and footage status

- FIFA's response distinguishes a tread on an opponent's foot from ball-first contact it judged normal. “Ball first” alone is not a universal exemption from foul play. The short can challenge the comparison while stating the explanation accurately.
- The strongest supported criticism is that viewers deserve a transparent, consistent intervention threshold. Comparing selected incidents does not by itself demonstrate a tournament-wide pattern; a broader claim would require systematically examining favorable and unfavorable decisions.
- Full-motion footage was not personally inspected during this research. Match events currently rest on official records and published descriptions. Before presenting an exact challenge reconstruction, inspect the complete build-ups, normal-speed action and available reverse angles; record broadcast timestamps separately from event minutes.
