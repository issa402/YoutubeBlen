## Research passes

### Current sentiment

Use last30days for the present backlash, recurring audience language, creator narratives, and engagement. Save raw results locally. Social engagement is not factual corroboration.

### Historical record

Use primary match records, full footage where lawfully available, official statements, competition regulations, and contemporaneous reporting.

### Incident review

For every officiating incident, record the full sequence, not only slow-motion clips. Capture the applicable law at that date and seek credible analysis supporting both interpretations.

### Competing-video review

Use yt-dlp only where authorized to obtain subtitles/audio for research. Use VibeVoice locally for transcripts. Analyze narrative structure, evidence and omissions; do not copy scripts or distinctive expression.
