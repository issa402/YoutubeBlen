# Episode 001 Brief

## Working title

**Why Is Messi Facing So Much Hate Right Now?**

Draft alternative titles immediately from the creator's angle. Lock factual and current-trend promises after checking the supporting evidence.

## Viewer promise

Explain why a player once treated as the tragic genius of Argentina is now described by some fans as protected, manufactured, or institutionally favored - and test how much of that story is supported by evidence.

## Narrative engine

Open in the present with the intensity of the backlash. Then ask the viewer to travel back to 2016, when Messi missed in the Copa America final, cried, and announced his international retirement. Present the apparent contradiction:

```text
2016: the hero who could not win with Argentina
2021-2024: the international champion who seemed unable to stop winning
Present: the legend whose success itself became suspicious to critics
```

The episode investigates what changed: Messi, Argentina, tournament structure, officiating decisions, institutional incentives, media narratives, or fan interpretation.

## Proposed thesis options

Select a provisional creator thesis before research. Test its factual premises while drafting; preserve the intended opinion and flag necessary factual corrections explicitly.

1. **Protection thesis:** Institutional incentives made Messi unusually valuable and may have influenced treatment around him.
2. **Perception thesis:** A cluster of favorable or disputed incidents, amplified by rivalry culture, created a corruption narrative stronger than the available evidence.
3. **Legacy-war thesis:** The backlash is primarily a reaction to Messi's victories settling the GOAT debate for many fans.
4. **Mixed thesis:** Real officiating controversies, institutional marketing incentives, and tribal interpretation reinforced one another without proving a coordinated fix.

These are options for the creator, not an instruction for the AI to default to the mixed thesis. The creator's final preference is not yet recorded.

## Tone

- Investigative but conversational
- Subjective commentary clearly labeled
- Suspense through unanswered questions
- Receipts visible on screen
- Fair counterarguments
- No fake certainty

## Target output

- First produce a 30-60 second visual proof of concept
- Then determine final episode length from the evidence and story, not a fixed quota
- Create a horizontal master; derive Shorts only after the long-form story works
